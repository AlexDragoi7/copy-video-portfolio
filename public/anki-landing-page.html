One Constellation at a Time

Anki makes complex astrophysical telemetry easy, so you can enjoy discovering space in a fun and immersive way. Powered by a clean interface and an ultra fast rendering engine, exploring exoplanets, nebulae or galaxies in spectacular details has never been so satisfying

Stellar features

*Anki is currently available only on iOS and macOS. Subscribe to our newsletter, so you can receive news and information about its launch on Android, Linux and Windows, as well as other upcoming features

📱

Sky Mapping & Interactive AR

Delve into planetary surfaces, examine natural satellites or your favorite star clusters. Simply point your phone at the night sky and let yourself be engaged in a deep space journey with our custom Augmented Reality features

🚀

Virtual Space Travel

Buckle up and traverse the space. Zoom in and out to view the observable universe or take an outstanding 3D guided tour through our solar system and interstellar space

☄️

Real-Time Astronomical Events

Your virtual planetarium. See beautifully rendered solar storms, eclipses and even supernova explosions. Also, interact with a planet and check its current environment, composition, rotational period and more

🎮

Gamified Learning

Learning is boring when not playable. Anki makes it interactive through daily challenges, quizzes and the ability to progress your level. A higher level brings more in-app rewards, unlocked new features and even cash prizes

🛰️

Asteroid Tracking

Join NASA and the European Space Agency in tracking more than 40,000 near-Earth asteroids. This feature lets you observe and keep note of trajectories, changing brightness or surface alterations. Share your analysis and become a real astronomer
